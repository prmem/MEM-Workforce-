# Employee Guide (Phase 1)

## First time
1. You will receive an email titled **"You have been invited to MEM Workforce"**.
2. Open the link and press **Continue**.
3. Choose a password with at least 8 characters, using letters and numbers.
4. You will see your dashboard. You can add the app to your phone's home screen from the browser menu ("Add to Home screen").

## Signing in
Use your work email and password. Forgot it? Press **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page. On a shared device, always **Sign out** from the **Profile** page when you finish.

## Your tasks
- **My Tasks** lists your work. Overdue tasks are marked in red.
- Open a task to see the details, then use **Update progress**:
  - **Start / continue work**: moves the task to *In Progress*.
  - **I am blocked**: explain what is stopping you in the Note.
  - **Quantity done so far**: record how much you have finished.
  - **Work is finished — submit for review**: add a note about what you did. Your manager will then **approve** it (Completed) or **reopen** it with a reason.
- While a task is waiting for review, you cannot change it.

## Your profile
**Profile** shows your details and lets you change your password. If any detail is wrong, ask your admin to correct it.

---

## For managers
- **My Team** shows the people who report directly to you.
- **Tasks → New task** assigns work to your team members only.
- Tasks your team submits appear under **Waiting for your review** on the dashboard. Open one and **Approve** or **Reopen** it (a reason is required).
- You can **edit**, **reassign** (within your team) or **cancel** (a reason is required) open tasks for your team.
- Only admins can change employee details.
