# Admin Guide (Phase 1)

## Signing in
Go to the app and sign in with your work email and password. On a shared computer, always **Sign out** (Profile page or the sidebar) when you finish.

## Adding an employee
1. **Employees → Add employee**.
2. Fill in Employee ID (must be unique), full name and work email. Department, designation, reporting manager, phone and joining date are optional.
3. Choose the **App role**:
   - **Employee**: sees only their own tasks and profile.
   - **Manager**: also sees and assigns tasks to people whose *Reporting manager* is them.
   - **Admin**: sees and manages everything.
4. Press **Add employee and send invite**. The employee receives an email with a link to set their password.
   - Didn't arrive? Open the employee and press **Resend invite**.
   - The work email cannot be changed after the account is created.

## Building teams
A manager's team is everyone whose **Reporting manager** is set to that manager. To move someone to another team, edit them and change the reporting manager. A manager who still has active team members cannot be made an Employee or deactivated. Move their team first.

## Deactivating
Open the employee and press **Deactivate employee**. They are signed out of all data immediately and cannot sign in again. Nothing is deleted, and their history stays. **Reactivate** restores access. You cannot deactivate yourself, and the last active admin cannot be removed.

## Departments
**Departments** lets you add, rename, reorder (the "Order" number) and deactivate departments. Departments are never deleted.

## Tasks
- **Tasks → New task**: title, person, priority, optional dates, optional target quantity and unit. If you give a due date without a time, the task is due by 11:59 PM that day.
- When an employee submits work, it appears under **Waiting for review** on the dashboard. Open it and press **Approve** (marks it Completed) or **Reopen** (a reason is required).
- **Edit task**, **Reassign** and **Cancel task** (a reason is required) are on the task page while it is open.
- Every change is kept in the task's **History**.

## Dashboard
It shows active employees, open, overdue and awaiting-review tasks, tasks by status, and employees per department. All numbers come straight from the database.
